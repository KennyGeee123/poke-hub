Support

Public support URL for store listings. In-app: open Settings (gear) or More for Privacy / Terms links.

Email kmitchjr7@gmail.com for account, collection sync, Scan, or privacy questions.

How do I track my collection?

Open Vault, add cards from Discover/Search/Sets, or Scan a card with the camera.

How do Buy / Sell work?

They deep-link to marketplaces (eBay, TCGplayer, etc.). PokéVault does not take payment for physical cards.

How do I delete my account?

Email kmitchjr7@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask for deletion.

Is this an official Pokémon app?

No. PokéVault is an unofficial fan app, not affiliated with Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, or Game Freak.
