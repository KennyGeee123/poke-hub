Unofficial fan app. PokéVault is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Game Freak, or related rights holders.

Who we are

PokéVault is a fan-made Pokémon TCG collection tracker, market helper, scanner, and playful Adventure mode. This policy covers the website, iOS app, and Android app.

Information you give us

Account details (email, password, or Sign in with Google / Apple identifiers). Collection data you save (Vault cards, quantities, wishlist). Optional pokemontcg.io API keys you paste (stored only on your device). Camera frames when you use Scan — for card identification in that session.

Information we get from others

Google and Apple may return a verified email and name. Catalog and price data come from third-party TCG sources. We do not sell personal data.

How we use it

To run your account, sync your collection, show prices, power Scan and Adventure progress, prevent abuse, and support you. Buy/Sell buttons only deep-link to external marketplaces.

Who we share it with

Supabase (auth + database) and the app host (Vercel). We share data if required by law or to protect safety. No sale of personal information; no cross-app advertising SDKs.

Tracking

PokéVault does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Camera

Camera access is used only when you choose Scan. We do not use the camera in the background.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and collection records while your account is open. Sign out anytime. Email kmitchjr7@gmail.com to request deletion of your account and server-side collection data.

Children

Not directed at children under 13. Contact us to request deletion if needed.
